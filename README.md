# Amazon-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard-Sales-Product-Performance
An interactive Amazon Sales Analysis dashboard designed to monitor YTD/QTD sales, product movement, customer reviews, category performance, monthly/weekly sales trends, and top-performing products.

# Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard

# Project Overview:

This project analyzes Amazon product sales performance and presents key business metrics through an interactive sales dashboard.

The objective is to help stakeholders monitor revenue performance, identify sales trends, compare product categories, and understand customer engagement through product reviews.

# Business Objectives:

The dashboard focuses on four key KPIs:

- YTD Sales – Monitor year-to-date revenue performance.
- QTD Sales – Track quarterly sales and identify sales fluctuations.
- YTD Products Sold – Analyze the total number of products sold throughout the year.
- YTD Reviews – Monitor customer reviews to understand customer engagement and feedback.

# Sales Analysis:

The dashboard includes:

- Monthly sales trend analysis
- Weekly sales performance
- Sales by product category
- YTD vs QTD category sales
- Percentage contribution by category
- Top 5 products by YTD sales
- Top 5 products by YTD reviews

# Product Categories:

The analysis covers categories including:

- Camera
- Car Accessories
- Men Clothes
- Men Shoes
- Mobile & Accessories
- Toys

The dashboard allows users to compare category-level performance and identify major contributors to overall sales.

# Key Business Insights:

- Men Shoes represents the largest share of YTD category sales, contributing approximately 43.18%.
- Camera contributes approximately 22.62% of YTD sales.
- Men Clothes contributes approximately 16.42%.
- Sales show noticeable variation across months, with stronger performance toward the later months of the year.
- Weekly sales analysis helps identify periods of increased sales activity.
- Product-level analysis highlights the highest-selling products and products receiving the most customer reviews.

# Dashboard Sections:

# 1. KPI Overview
Provides a quick view of YTD Sales, QTD Sales, Products Sold, and Reviews.

# 2. Sales by Month
Shows monthly sales trends and helps identify periods of growth and decline.

# 3. Sales by Week
Provides a detailed view of weekly sales movement.

# 4. Sales by Product Category
Compares YTD sales, QTD sales, and percentage contribution across product categories.

# 5. Top 5 Products by YTD Sales
Identifies products generating the highest sales.

# 6. Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews
Highlights products with the highest number of customer reviews.
